📌 Project Overview

The Financial Intelligence Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of an organization's financial performance by tracking key metrics such as Sales, Profit, Profit Margin, Units Sold, Country Performance, Product Performance, and Sales Trends.

The dashboard is designed for executives and business managers to quickly identify growth opportunities, monitor profitability, and evaluate business performance through interactive visualizations.

🎯 Business Objectives
Monitor overall financial performance.
Track sales and profitability across countries.
Analyze product performance.
Identify the most profitable market segments.
Compare monthly sales and profit trends.
Monitor profit margin over time.
Enable interactive filtering for detailed analysis.

📌 Dashboard Insights
🌍 Regional Performance
France is the highest revenue-generating country with $24.35M in sales.
Mexico and Japan are the next strongest contributors.
The top five countries contribute a significant share of total revenue.
📦 Product Performance
Paseo is the best-selling product.
VTT and Velo follow as the next highest-performing products.
Product-level analysis helps identify high-demand products.
👥 Customer Segment Analysis
Government segment contributes the highest sales.
Small Business is the second-largest customer segment.
Midmarket and Enterprise contribute moderate revenue.
Channel Partners account for a relatively smaller share.
📈 Monthly Performance
Sales remain relatively stable throughout the year.
Profit peaks during October and December.
Seasonal fluctuations can support inventory and sales planning.
💰 Profitability
Overall Profit Margin is 14.23%.
Profit Margin fluctuates monthly, with the highest performance observed in December.
Lower margins during some months indicate opportunities for cost optimization.
📊 Financial Flow Analysis

The waterfall chart illustrates the financial journey from:

Gross Sales → Net Sales → COGS → Profit

This visualization clearly explains how discounts and costs impact overall profitability.
